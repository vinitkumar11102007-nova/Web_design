Welcome to the GL Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management Online Bookstore

The GL Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management Online Bookstore is a one-stop web portal for students to browse, search and purchase books recommended across the CSE, ECE, EEE and CIVIL departments. Use the department links on the left to view books relevant to your branch, or use the navigation bar above to log in, register a new account, view the full catalogue, or check your shopping cart.

This site was built as part of the BCS353 - Web Designing Workshop lab, demonstrating HTML frames, forms, tables, navigation bars and CSS styling.
